# PunëMbaruar — Provider Verification Policy (v1.4)

## Rule
Self-registration does not equal approval. New providers remain inactive until PunëMbaruar approves them.

## Required onboarding
- business/service name and categories
- responsible person
- WhatsApp/phone
- business address and service area
- NIPT when operating as a registered business
- map/location link
- minimum 3 REQUIRED photos: exterior/sign, work area, relevant equipment/work zone
- video OPTIONAL by default; may be requested by Admin
- additional documents only when requested

## Workflow
APPLICATION → DOCUMENTS_SUBMITTED / UNDER_REVIEW → APPROVED
Alternative states: MORE_INFO_REQUIRED, REJECTED, SUSPENDED.

Only APPROVED providers are active and eligible for matching/WhatsApp request notifications.

## Verification badges
- IDENTITY_VERIFIED
- BUSINESS_VERIFIED
- VISITED_VERIFIED

Physical inspection is risk-based, not mandatory for every provider. It can be required for suspicious/incomplete applications or used to award the stronger “Vizituar & Verifikuar” badge.

## Reviews
Keep reviews tied to completed marketplace jobs. Do not create open, anonymous reviews.

## Privacy
Verification evidence is operational/private data. Public profiles should show the resulting verification badge, not private documents.


## Submission gate v1.4.1
An application cannot enter UNDER_REVIEW until NIPT, responsible person, business address and at least 3 PHOTO evidence records are present. Video is not required by default. Physical visit remains Admin-controlled.
