# PunëMbaruar — Launch Product Strategy

## Fokus
Launch-i është automotive-first. Premtimi kryesor:
**Ke problem me makinën? Përshkruaje një herë. Merr oferta nga serviset.**

## Mekanizmat e inkorporuar
### Nga Openbay
- service OR symptom/diagnosis flow
- vehicle garage/history foundation
- quote comparison by price + reviews + provider profile
- parts/labor transparency
- warranty and appointment availability
- provider verification concept
- quote types: fixed, range, diagnostic required

### Nga KërkoPjesë
- extremely low-friction parts request
- new/used preference, part code, seller offer model
- private competing offers
- marketplace safety principle: real requests and real offers

### Nga myUSTA
- verified provider profiles
- ratings/reviews
- matching by profession/category and location
- provider growth dashboard / reputation accumulation

## PunëMbaruar differentiation
Problem → matching → WhatsApp notification → structured quote → compare → choose → chat → job → review.
Automotive services and spare parts coexist in the same customer garage/workflow.

## Launch categories
1. Problem / Repair
2. Spare Parts
3. Diagnosis
4. Tires
5. A/C
6. Body / Paint

General home services remain architecturally available but are not the launch message.

## Trust rules for launch
- Verified badge is manual/admin-controlled.
- Reviews only after a completed job.
- Providers state warranty and parts type.
- No promise of a fixed price when diagnosis is required.
- Request media supports photos/video.
- Contact exposure should happen after meaningful marketplace interaction.
