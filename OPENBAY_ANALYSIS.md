# Openbay → PunëMbaruar launch analysis

Studied from Openbay's official public materials (September 2026).

## What is worth incorporating
- User can select a known service OR describe symptoms / request diagnosis.
- Vehicle details are central to the request.
- Multiple providers can be compared on price, reviews, distance and convenience.
- Quotes should be transparent and can include parts + labor.
- Provider profile/reputation and vetting matter.
- Service history belongs to the vehicle/account.
- Messaging stays inside the marketplace.
- Provider responsiveness should affect marketplace quality/ranking.

## What PunëMbaruar should NOT copy blindly
- Albania launch should not require a giant predefined repair catalog.
- Do not promise an exact fixed repair price when a physical diagnosis is necessary.
- Do not add online payments/escrow in launch MVP.
- Do not require email before a customer can request quotes.
- Do not over-vet so aggressively at launch that supply cannot grow; use staged verification.

## PunëMbaruar automotive launch proposition
“Ke problem me makinën? Përshkruaje një herë dhe merr oferta nga serviset.”

Six launch entry points:
1. Problem / repair
2. Spare parts
3. Diagnosis
4. Tires / tire shop
5. Air conditioning
6. Body / paint

## Quote model
A shop can respond with:
- fixed total price;
- price range;
- diagnostic fee, with final repair price after inspection.

Optional quote breakdown:
- parts;
- labor;
- warranty;
- estimated duration;
- appointment availability.

This is deliberately safer and more credible than forcing every shop to provide a fixed price for an unknown fault.
